Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy is short because this site collects almost nothing. That is deliberate. We advise companies on protecting data, so we start by not gathering yours.

What this site collects

Very little. There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no accounts on controlandfunction.com. The one form is the “Ask a question” box, described below. We use GoatCounter for aggregate, cookie-free analytics: page counts, referrer, browser type, and country. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint devices, and does not collect personal information. We cannot identify you from it and do not try.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it receives the standard details of each request, including your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are served from this site itself, so loading a page sends nothing to Google or any other font service.

If you use “Ask a question”

The “Ask a question” button on each page opens a short form. It collects your message and, only if you choose to give them, your name, your email address, and how you found us. It also notes which page you sent it from. The form sends this to a small function we run on Vercel, which relays it by email through Resend to the firm's inbox. The function does not save it anywhere. We use it to answer you and to conduct any engagement that follows, the same as an email.

If you email us

If you write to hello@controlandfunction.com, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it to reply and to conduct any engagement that follows. We do not add you to marketing lists, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone.

If you book a call

Scheduling runs on Calendly. The information you enter there (name, email, meeting details) is processed by Calendly under Calendly's privacy notice. We receive the booking details and use them to hold the meeting.

Client engagements

Consulting engagements are governed by their own agreements, which take precedence over this policy for engagement materials. As a matter of practice we track the status of client compliance programs and pointers to evidence, and we do not take custody of raw sensitive artifacts, which remain in the client's own environment.

Retention and your rights

Email correspondence, including messages sent through “Ask a question”, is retained as long as needed for the relationship it belongs to. If you want correspondence deleted, ask and we will delete it unless a legal or contractual obligation requires otherwise. To exercise any privacy right, email hello@controlandfunction.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. Given how little this site collects, changes should be rare.
